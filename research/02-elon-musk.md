## Ancestry trunk (brown)

**Maternal (Canada → South Africa):** Joshua N. Haldeman (1902–1974), chiropractor/aviator, Canadian director of **Technocracy Incorporated**, later **Social Credit** national chair, moved the family to South Africa in 1950. *The Atlantic* (Joshua Benton, 2023) documents antisemitic, anti-democratic, apartheid-supporting writings. Wife Winnifred “Wyn” Fletcher. Daughter **Maye** (b. 19 Apr 1948, Regina). This is a **grandfather node**, not an automatic political edge onto Elon (Joshua died when Elon was two).

**Paternal:** Walter Henry James Musk (SA WWII veteran) and Cora Amelia Robinson (Liverpool-born) → **Errol Graham Musk** (b. 25 May 1946), engineer, Pretoria city councillor, later Progressive Federal Party. Married Maye 1970–1979. Children: Elon, **Kimbal** (b. 1972), **Tosca** (b. 1974). Errol later married Heide Bezuidenhout; daughters Alexandra and Asha Rose. Errol’s later relationship with former stepdaughter **Jana Bezuidenhout** produced more children — **Errol’s**, not Elon’s.

**Cousin:** **Lyndon Rive** (SolarCity) via Maye’s twin sister Kaye.
