# Web 1 — Entertainment industry (bounded)

**Status:** sourced seed graph, not a census of Hollywood.  
**Bound:** three interlocking clusters — (A) Coppola / Fonda / Huston dynasties, (B) 1947 HUAC hearings, Hollywood Ten, Waldorf Statement, Committee for the First Amendment, (C) New Hollywood / American Zoetrope. A person is in the graph only with a documented edge to someone else in it.

**Not in this seed (good later expansions):** Scientology celebrity membership (Cruise, Travolta, etc.) is a real religion-string cluster but does not currently share a high-confidence edge with the Coppola–Fonda–Huston–blacklist core. The Barrymore, Sheen/Estevez, Redgrave, and Douglas-beyond-Kirk families are likewise real dynasties; Kirk Douglas is in because he is the public credit-break for Dalton Trumbo on *Spartacus*.

## How the corkboard should read

Pin the **dynasties** as family trees (brown). Overlay **red** for the marriages that jump trees (Jane Fonda–Tom Hayden, Anjelica Huston–Jack Nicholson, Sofia Coppola–Spike Jonze). Overlay **forest** for Zoetrope, SAG, the Academy, the studios that signed the Waldorf policy. Overlay **navy** for HUAC vs. CFA vs. CPUSA. Overlay **ivory** for the Waldorf Statement, HUAC transcripts, and the CFA’s paid trade-paper ads. Overlay **gold** for documented Catholic Coppola/Huston-Ireland heritage and for the several Hollywood Ten members who were Jewish — as heritage, not as a plot.

The visual trick of a conspiracy board is that these layers coincide. That coincidence is the history of a small industry, not proof of a secret.

## Cluster A — Dynasties

### Coppola (Italian Catholic immigrant family → film)

Agostino Coppola and Maria Zasa emigrated from Bernalda, Basilicata, and married in New York on 25 January 1908. Two of their sons, **Carmine Coppola** (1910–1991) and **Anton Coppola** (1917–2020), became composers/conductors. Carmine married **Italia Pennino**; their children are **August Coppola** (professor), **Francis Ford Coppola** (b. 1939), and **Talia Shire** (b. 1946, née Talia Rose Coppola).

Francis married **Eleanor Neil** (1936–2024); their children **Gian-Carlo** (1963–1986), **Roman**, and **Sofia** all worked in film. Gian-Carlo’s daughter **Gia Coppola** continues as a director. Sofia was married to **Spike Jonze** (1999–2003) and later to **Thomas Mars** of Phoenix.

August’s son **Nicolas Kim Coppola** (b. 1964) acts as **Nicolas Cage** — a name change he has said was to avoid the appearance of nepotism. Talia married composer **David Shire**, then producer **Jack Schwartzman**; sons **Jason Schwartzman** and **Robert Schwartzman**.

Lineal descendants of Carmine and Italia have 8 Oscar wins / 24 nominations among Carmine, Francis, Nicolas Cage, and Sofia. In-laws David Shire, Spike Jonze, and Patricia Arquette (Cage’s ex-wife) are also Oscar winners.

Francis and **George Lucas** co-founded **American Zoetrope** in San Francisco (1969). Zoetrope is the organizational hub that pulls Lucas, and through him New Hollywood, onto this board.

Sources: [Coppola family (Wikipedia, cited genealogy)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coppola_family); [American Zoetrope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Zoetrope).

### Fonda (Dutch-American acting line + New Left marriage)

**Henry Jaynes Fonda** (1905–1982), born Grand Island, Nebraska; Democrat. Five marriages, including actress **Margaret Sullavan** (1931–33) and socialite **Frances Ford Seymour** (1936 until her death in 1950). Jane (b. 1937) and Peter (1940–2019) are Frances’s children.

Jane’s marriages: director **Roger Vadim** (1965–73; daughter Vanessa Vadim), activist/legislator **Tom Hayden** (1973–90; son **Troy Garity**, unofficially adopted **Mary Luana Williams**), then **Ted Turner** (1991–2001). Peter’s daughter **Bridget Fonda** married composer **Danny Elfman**.

Henry Fonda flew to Washington in October 1947 with the **Committee for the First Amendment** to protest HUAC — the political string that ties this dynasty to Cluster B.

Sources: [Henry Fonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Fonda); [Jane Fonda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Fonda); HUAC/CFA coverage in [Hollywood blacklist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_blacklist).

### Huston (three Oscar generations + 17-year Nicholson partnership)

**Walter Huston** (actor) → **John Huston** (1906–1987, director/writer/actor) → **Anjelica Huston** (b. 1951), **Tony**, **Danny**, **Allegra**. John directed Walter to an Oscar in *The Treasure of the Sierra Madre* (1948) and Anjelica to an Oscar in *Prizzi’s Honor* (1985), with **Jack Nicholson** starring.

John Huston co-founded the Committee for the First Amendment with Philip Dunne and William Wyler (Anjelica’s memoir *A Story Lately Told* and standard HUAC histories). Anjelica’s on/off partnership with Nicholson ran **1973–1990** (her memoir *Watch Me*; contemporaneous coverage). That red string plus *Easy Rider* (Nicholson with **Peter Fonda**) is the Fonda–Huston bridge.

John, disgusted by the blacklist, later based himself in Ireland (St. Clerans). Treat “Irish Catholic heritage / Ireland residence” as a soft gold string, not a parish-roll fact unless a baptismal source is added later.

Sources: [John Huston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Huston); [Anjelica Huston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anjelica_Huston); NY Post / VF on the Nicholson years.

## Cluster B — Blacklist, 1947–1960

### Hollywood Ten

Subpoenaed autumn 1947; cited for contempt of Congress 24 November 1947 (House vote 346–17); most served 6–12 months. They refused to answer whether they were CPUSA members, citing the First Amendment.

AJHS notes **six of the Ten were Jewish**, as were many of the studio executives who then voted to fire them. That is a gold-string fact on both sides of the Waldorf table, not a one-sided cabal.

### Waldorf Statement (ivory + forest + navy)

On **25 November 1947**, after a closed meeting of ~48 executives at the Waldorf-Astoria, MPAA president **Eric Johnston** issued the press release now called the Waldorf Statement. Core pledges:

1. Discharge or suspend the Ten without pay; no re-employment until contempt is purged **and** they swear they are not Communists.
2. “We will not knowingly employ a Communist or a member of any party or group which advocates the overthrow of the government of the United States by force…”

Attendees included studio chiefs (Warner, Mayer/MGM, Cohn/Columbia, Schary/RKO among those named in trade coverage). **Dore Schary, Samuel Goldwyn, Walter Wanger, and Eddie Mannix** are reported as dissenters inside the room who still signed onto the public statement.

Source: [Waldorf Statement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waldorf_Statement); [Deadline 75th anniversary](https://deadline.com/2022/11/hollywood-blacklist-anniversary-waldorf-conference-1235180135/); [Hollywood Reporter, 2017](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/reflections-hollywoods-infamous-blacklist-70-years-guest-column-1060628/).

### Committee for the First Amendment (navy + organization)

Huston, Wyler, Dunne; members who flew to Washington included **Humphrey Bogart, Lauren Bacall, Gene Kelly, Danny Kaye, Jane Wyatt**, and others. **Henry Fonda** is named among CFA protesters in standard blacklist histories. After hostile press, Bogart published a distancing piece (“As Bogart Sees It Now”). The CFA is the documented **anti-HUAC** political cluster.

Friendly HUAC witnesses in October 1947 included **Walt Disney, Jack L. Warner, Gary Cooper, Ronald Reagan** (then SAG president), **Robert Taylor, Adolphe Menjou**. Reagan testified to a “small clique” using “communist-like tactics” in SAG without naming them as party members.

### Break of the blacklist (1960)

**Otto Preminger** hired Trumbo openly for *Exodus* (1960). **Kirk Douglas** publicly credited Trumbo for *Spartacus* (1960). That is the Douglas–Trumbo organization/communications bridge into Cluster A’s neighboring generation (Michael Douglas is optional later).

### Other political orgs on this board

- **CPUSA** — membership alleged or admitted for several of the Ten (Trumbo: member 1943–48 per standard bios).
- **Screen Actors Guild** — loyalty pledge 17 November 1947; Reagan as president.
- **Screen Writers Guild** — 1952 rule letting studios omit names of those not cleared by Congress.
- **Motion Picture Alliance for the Preservation of American Ideals** — Ayn Rand’s *Screen Guide for Americans*.
- **Red Channels** (1950) — 151 broadcasting names; not fully ingested here.

## Cluster C — New Hollywood / Zoetrope

American Zoetrope (Coppola + Lucas, 1969) produced or incubated *THX 1138*, *American Graffiti* (Coppola produced; Lucas directed; Coppola Oscar-nominated as producer), and Coppola’s 1970s run (*The Godfather*, *The Conversation*, *Apocalypse Now*). **Talia Shire** plays Connie Corleone in *The Godfather* films (she has said she auditioned under “Shire” so the sister-link was not obvious). **Sofia Coppola** was widely attacked as nepotism casting in *The Godfather Part III*; she moved to directing (*Lost in Translation*, Oscar for original screenplay).

**Jack Nicholson** in *Easy Rider* (1969) with **Peter Fonda** is the New Hollywood red/org crossover from Cluster A.

## Uncertainties (do not pin)

- Exact CPUSA card dates for each of the Ten except where a bio states them (Trumbo 1943–48 is the cleanest).
- Whether every CFA celebrity on popular lists actually boarded the plane; use contemporaneous passenger lists before adding more names.
- Scientology, Kabbalah Centre, Bohemian Grove: real-or-rumored Hollywood orgs that are **out of scope** until a sourced edge hits this core.
- Studio-founder Jewishness (Warner, Mayer, Goldwyn, Zukor) is historically true and often misused; it is omitted from the seed until the graph has a specific person-to-person reason to include those founders (e.g. Jack Warner’s HUAC-friendly testimony).

- Nicolas Cage’s adult religious self-description (has discussed Catholicism, other interests) — family gold string is enough.
